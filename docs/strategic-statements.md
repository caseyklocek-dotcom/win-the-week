# Win the Week — Strategic Statements

*Drafted June 25, 2026. Voice follows `../../Casey-Voice.md`. These are living statements; tighten as the business clarifies.*

---

## VISION

Christian leaders free to disciple the people in front of them, raise up the leaders coming behind them, and carry the gospel into every place they lead.

---

## MISSION

We are relentless about giving Christian leaders their time back. We find the hours the weekly grind is stealing and return them, and so a leader is free to spend them on the people and the calling in front of them.

---

## CORE CONVICTION

Sunday is a celebration of what God did all week. Worship happens Monday through Saturday, in homes and workplaces and neighborhoods, and so Sunday is the family gathering to celebrate it together. We build everything on this conviction, and so we measure a win by the fullness of a leader's whole week.

---

## VALUES

**PROTECT THE SABBATH.** Rest is built into the design, and so we treat it as part of the work itself. We keep a start and an end to the day, we keep work inside its hours, and we leave it there when we go home. A leader who is rested leads for the long haul.

**KEEP THE ORDER.** God first, then family, then community, then work. We hold that order on purpose, and so the work stays in its right place. Every tool we build and every conversation we lead is meant to protect it.

**TELL THE TRUTH.** We say what is true about the work, the results, and the price. We show our methods in the open and we let real outcomes speak before we make a claim. Trust is the whole business, and so we guard it with honesty.

**PROFIT FOR THE KINGDOM.** We aim to run a profitable business so the kingdom is funded by it. The margin is meant to move, out into churches, into leaders, into the spread of the gospel. We measure success by how much good the profit goes on to do.
