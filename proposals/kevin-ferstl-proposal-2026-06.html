The Coaching
The Two Intensives

First You Win Your Week Back. Then You Invest It.

The coaching comes in two four-week intensives, the shorter, phased shape you asked for. The whole thing balances the push and the pull: first the leader gets their week back so they can rest and lead from a full cup, and then they spend that reclaimed time on the people God gave them. Most leaders take them one at a time, with several months in between. Intensive 1 is a complete win on its own.
